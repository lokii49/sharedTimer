• Tags and notes: tag timers like Kitchen or Work, filter the list, and add a note that comes back the next time you start that timer.
• Stats: History groups repeat runs, and a new Stats page shows your week, month and streaks, with export to a spreadsheet.
• Test my alarm, in Settings, rings a real alarm in five seconds and shows what to change if it doesn't get through.
• A new − button takes time off a timer, also in Messages, on Apple Watch and with Siri.
• Messages: edit a timer, start a saved sequence, make a countdown repeat, add heads-up reminders and undo a removal.
• Spoken cues for sequences: phase names, a one-minute warning and beeps while Timer is open.
• Focus filters: keep reminders and shared-timer updates quiet during a Focus.
• Apple Watch: a corner complication with the time left and a draining arc.
• Shortcuts can find timers by name, kind or state, and a pause or extend asked of Siri can be undone.
• Days since: count the days since something, and a stopwatch with laps, both under +.
• Give a timer your own photo as its background.
• Four new alarm tones: Chime, Rise, Bloom and Marimba.
• A quiet sequence set to start within the next 7 hours shows its Live Activity on time, even with Timer closed.
• An overtime count now stops when you dismiss the full-screen alarm.
• Plus many smaller fixes.
