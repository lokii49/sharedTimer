Timer Countdown & Share

Get in touch

Questions, a bug, or an idea for Timer? Email hello@mirrornotes.org. It helps to mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the Timer version (Settings → About).

Common questions

  • A shared timer doesn't update for the other person. Live sharing uses iCloud. Both people need to be signed in to iCloud; the share screen says “Live” when the link stays in sync and “Snapshot” when it won't.
  • The alarm didn't ring. Allow Timer's alarms when asked (Settings → Apps → Timer). Alarms sound through the silent switch and Focus once allowed.
  • Who has my shared timer? Open the timer, tap Share, then Manage Sharing to see people, remove someone or stop sharing.
  • Change my name or alarm tone. Tap the gear next to + on the Timers screen.
